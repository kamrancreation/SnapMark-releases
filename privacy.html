Privacy policy

Your screenshots stay on your PC

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The short version

SnapMark doesn't collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There's no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you capture and edit stays on your computer.

What SnapMark does on your computer

  • Screenshots are captured, edited and saved only on your PC, in the folder you choose (by default, Pictures\SnapMark). They're copied to your clipboard only when you ask.
  • Text recognition (Grab text and Auto-redact) uses the OCR engine built into Windows and runs entirely on your PC. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Settings such as your shortcuts, theme, editor preferences and the contact details you type for client reports are saved in a settings file on your PC and are never sent anywhere.

Network use

SnapMark doesn't send your screenshots or any personal information over the internet.

  • Microsoft Store version: updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store, under Microsoft's privacy statement. SnapMark itself makes no network requests.
  • Version downloaded from this website: SnapMark checks GitHub for new versions (a standard web request to github.com, which can see your IP address, like any website you visit). No personal data is included in the request.

Children

SnapMark doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact the developer through the SnapMark support page.
